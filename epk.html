Osos Discos is the dynamic DJ duo of Cameron Kelly and Marshall T, blending decades of experience with fresh passion on the decks. Based in Philadelphia, this partnership unites the soulful roots of New Orleans with the contemporary energy of Philly's dance scene. Their sound is all about feel-good vibes — a rich mix of classic house grooves and shimmering disco beats that light up any dance floor. Both DJs share a love for infectious rhythms and an unshakeable commitment to keeping the crowd moving, creating an atmosphere where everyone feels the groove.

01 — Two Decades Behind the Decks

Cameron Kelly

Cameron Kelly brings over 20 years of DJing expertise to Osos Discos. A New Orleans native who started throwing parties in 1996, Cameron has honed his craft across the country. He cut his teeth in the vibrant NOLA club circuit, holding down residencies at legendary spots like The Ninth Circle, Oz, The Bourbon Pub/Parade, and The Dragon's Den.

Over the years, he expanded into radio, with shows on pioneering internet stations such as ChicagoHouseFM, MyHouseYourHouse, and Sugar Shack Radio. After relocating to Philadelphia, Cameron became a familiar name in the local scene with regular sets at venues ranging from the iconic Bob & Barbara's Lounge to indie hotspots like Johnny Brenda's and the Richmond Street Flea. He's even brought his beats to Atlantic City, grooving at Superfrico and Angeloni's Club Madrid.

02 — From Roadie to Resident DJ

Marshall T

Marshall T is the newer half of Osos Discos, bringing a fresh spark and sharp ear to the duo's performances. He began his musical journey as Cameron's dedicated roadie (and husband), absorbing the craft from the sidelines as he helped lug vinyl and set up gear.

Inspired by Cameron's passion and the magic of the dance floor, Marshall soon tried his hand behind the decks. He quickly proved he had the talent to match his enthusiasm. Now an emerging DJ in his own right, Marshall holds down regular gigs at Superfrico, Angeloni's Club Madrid, and the Richmond Street Flea. He's also made crowd-pleasing appearances at various one-off events across Philadelphia, steadily building a name for his blend of upbeat disco classics and modern house tracks. Marshall's selections showcase his love for the genre's roots and his knack for finding the perfect track to elevate the mood.

United by Philly Roots

Together as Osos Discos, Cameron and Marshall deliver an irresistible energy born from their unique partnership. Their Philly roots run deep — the duo calls the City of Brotherly Love home, and their sets often pay homage to Philadelphia's rich musical heritage while also channeling the funky Crescent City flavors from Cameron's past. When they hit the booth side by side, you can expect a collaborative vibe where each DJ pushes the other to new heights. They seamlessly trade tracks, blending old-school with new-school, and their chemistry behind the decks is palpable.

Osos Discos thrives on creating an inclusive, jubilant atmosphere. Whether it's a packed nightclub, an outdoor block party, or a trendy lounge, they transform any space into a joyous dance floor. The duo's combined energy invites dancers of all ages and backgrounds to share in the groove. With Cameron's veteran instincts guiding the flow and Marshall's fresh perspective keeping things exciting, an Osos Discos set is a celebration of house and disco in its purest form.

Osos Discos isn't just two DJs playing back to back — Cameron and Marshall build and throw their own parties together. They co-founded Soulful Sessions Philly alongside Antonio Estrella; after his passing, they launched Pure House with Rogue, a residency that was later rebranded ReVibe when Rogue stepped away. They also run The Fishbowl Project, a feel-good, ego-free, body-positive, all-vinyl disco party held every third Sunday in Philadelphia, and have been featured together on WKDU 91.7FM's Freak Flag, the station's weekly electronic music guest-DJ program.
